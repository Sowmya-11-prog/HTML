About college

Aditya University in Surampalem, Kakinada district, Andhra Pradesh, isa state private university established under the AP Private Universities Act, 2016. Evolved from the prominent Aditya Engineering College (founded in 2001), it offers multidisciplinary undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, technology, and management with an A++ NAAC rating
